# No Trace is Discovered of Synka heiress But Search Continues

[[Marion Marigold|Mary Matthew Jones]], the author and illustrator whose fanciful stories are written under the pen name Marion Marigold, was last seen by a close friend around 3:45pm Friday getting into a carriage after a late luncheon date at the [[Hundred Bells Tea Room]] in [[Trinsdale]]. Mrs. Jones never arrived home and the carriage driver cannot be located. Family and local authorities are very concerned but have not deemed the case foul play. "It's an out-of-character incident at this point and we are seeking information"
